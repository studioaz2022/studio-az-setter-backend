## What actually explains each competitor

### Minneapolis Tattoo Shop (the 72% dominator)
1. **Their business name IS the keyword.** "Minneapolis Tattoo Shop" + matching
   exact-match domain (minneapolistattooshop.com). Keywords-in-title is the #3
   ranking factor industry-wide. This alone is unreplicable without a rename.
2. **A review machine.** 4–5 new reviews per week, every one answered. Review
   texts name the artist ("Nikki was incredible", "Teal has the softest hands") —
   artists are clearly asking clients at the chair, and it compounds: recency +
   velocity + artist-name keywords in review text.
3. **Their website is mediocre** — no style pages, no blog, external booking
   (getporter.io), no pricing, unnamed artists. The moat is NOT the site.
   Ours is materially better; the gap is profile prominence, not web.

### Sacred Siren (632 reviews)
- **Piercing is a review-volume engine.** Dual model (tattoo + piercing) means
  high-frequency, low-ticket visits that generate reviews constantly (most recent
  reviews are piercing reviews, incl. kids' ear piercing). Their local SEO is
  otherwise weak (no location keywords, hidden roster). Not a playbook we can
  copy without adding piercing.

### Citadel (the proof-of-concept: 88 reviews → 21.5% SoLV)
- "Walk Ins Welcome!" **stuffed into the GBP title** (as do Tiger Rose and
  Nokomis — 3 of the top shops violate the title guideline).
- Perfect 5.0, slow but **never-stalling** velocity (~2–3/month for years).
- Zero owner responses, thin website, NE neighborhood grid position.
- **Lesson: ~90 reviews at 5.0 with steady flow is enough to contest 20% of the
  map.** The bar for Studio AZ is ~100 steady reviews, not 500.

## Studio AZ's actual position
- 17 reviews, newest 2 months old — the tattoo side's review flow is stalled
  (barbershop's automated texts produce 540; tattoo's equivalent is either not
  wired or not firing). Recent reviews skew Spanish/Joan — organic, not prompted.
- We respond 8/8 (already best-in-class habit; keep).
- Website + GBP services (18 as of today) are now stronger than everyone except
  arguably none — MTS's site is weaker than ours.
- Both "walk-in" title stuffers rank well, but title keywords violate Google's
  guidelines (suspension risk) — observed, not recommended.

## Replicable playbook, in order
1. **Un-stall tattoo review flow** — wire the same post-visit SMS the barbershop
   uses to tattoo session completions (and consults that convert). Have artists
   ask at the chair; the review naming the artist is the pattern every winner
   shows. Target: 3–5/month steady → ~100 total by mid-2027 (Citadel bar).
2. **Keep 100% owner responses** (already doing).
3. **Exploit their content gap**: MTS has no style pages — our style/portfolio
   pages can own long-tail organic ("fine line tattoo minneapolis" etc.) while
   maps prominence builds.
4. Photos velocity via portfolio pipeline (visual-industry ranking factor).
5. Title keywords: decision explicitly left to Lionel; default = don't.
